I get that bandwidth has to be paid and all but there's so many other options other than locking music playing behind the paywall.

I'm not super fond of GitHub either, don't get me wrong, but it doesn't feel as restrictive with what you can do regarding your site. That and if I can't figure out how to do something, the better programmer in the house can take a look at the code and fix it.
