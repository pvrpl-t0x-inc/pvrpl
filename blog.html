Emotional labor and shitty bosses [Long Read]

Welp, after about 3 months of working at a call center, I quit.

For context, I worked for an outsourcing company on their AOL project (fuck both of them btw). I was a "services agent", A.K.A I took calls and made people buy bloatware so I could maybe fix the issue. Oh and 99% of the callers were entitled boomers.

Read more...

Y'see, working with entitled pricks is already a pain when you're a confrontational bitch such as myself. But then you have to give them "concierge treatment" (kiss their asses 'til you reach their spleen), you also gotta shove shit like McAffee down their throat, and if they treat you like shit you have to smile and move on (unless they insult you directly, in which case you can hang up on them).

I wasn't an angel, I'll admit that much. I did some less than ideal stuff (i.e I ragebaited a Bible thumper). However, I think that pales in comparison to what our superiors were putting us through.

We had to ask for our 15min breaks through a google form, it was approved on an excel on the basis of how long you'd spent without a break or if you're fucking the RTA, if you somehow missed a break due to a call taking long, well, skill issue it's your fault no we're not understaffed what's wrong with you. I've had multiple times where I had less than 12h between shifts, I wasn't properly notified of when they needed overtime from us and put on the spot multiple times regarding the overtime. Oh and I had to request to be excused multiple times because I was having some of the worst mental breakdowns in years.

Quitting wasn't a fun or easy decision, mind you. I'm fully aware of how that messed me up financially, but after some... less than ideal experiences I realized that I'd rather be broke for a bit rather than spiral down into suicidality.

So I went to the project manager before the start of my shift and told him all that. His answer? That I'm immature and selfish and that I'm messing up everyone's schedules because I couldn't wait 2 days and that this decision will also blacklist me from the company in the future.

He was fully fucking serious too. Had I been slightly less hinged that day, I would've ripped into him for the illegal bullshit he keeps doing regarding the breaks and the days off. Oh and his affair with one of the team leaders. I didn't, which I kinda regret. I'm blacklisted from that company either way so... suck my dick Adrian.

Now hosted on GitHub!

As you may've noticed, the blog's now gosted on GitHub instead of NeoCities.

So I really enjoyed the free hosting options that NeoCities offers, and it's super cool that there's free self-host options that at least somewhat bring back old web's creativity, but having the ability to add music locked behind a paywall is a little... eh? I get that bandwidth has to be paid and all but there's so many other options other than locking music playing behind the paywall.

I'm not super fond of GitHub either, don't get me wrong, but it doesn't feel as restrictive with what you can do regarding your site. That and if I can't figure out how to do something, the better programmer in the house can take a look at the code and fix it.
